Profile

I’m ookamitai, a technical creator interested in the space between computation and creative practice.

I build and study experimental systems around artificial intelligence, graphics, software, and sound. Current interests include machine learning, local language-model systems, generative tools, graphics programming, reverse engineering, open-source software, rhythm games, and music production.

I prefer small systems that can be inspected, understood, and changed. Much of my work begins as a question: how does this behave, what is hidden underneath it, and what happens if the parts are rearranged?

This site is a working index rather than a complete résumé.

Music systems

Music is another form of systems work: timing, structure, texture, and controlled variation.

My musical interests center on rhythm-game composition, Artcore, Jazz Fusion, UTAU, and sound design. I’m interested in how dense arrangements remain readable, how rhythm creates physical momentum, and how synthetic voices acquire character.
